MicChain

Free · Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) · Version 1.0

Download MicChainFree · .zip for Windows

What can you do with it?

MicChain runs your microphone through a chain of VST3 plugins - a denoiser, compressor, EQ, de-esser, whatever you like - and sends the result to a virtual cable. Every app that uses that cable as its microphone gets your processed sound: speech-to-text, Discord, OBS, calls.

  • Your own plugin chain - add any VST3 plugin, reorder, switch on/off, double-click to open.
  • Monitor - hear exactly what other apps hear while you tweak.
  • Presets - different chains for recording, calls or speech-to-text.
  • Auto-switch - change preset automatically when a program like OBS starts.
  • Shortcuts - Ctrl+Alt+1-9 switches presets from anywhere.
  • Runs in the background - lives in the tray, can start with Windows.

How to install

  1. Install VB-Cable (free) and restart your PC.
  2. Download MicChain with the button above and unzip it to any folder.
  3. Double-click MicChain.exe.
  4. Pick your microphone, keep Send to on CABLE Input, add plugins and press Start.
  5. In the app you want to use (Discord, OBS, speech-to-text…), choose CABLE Output as the microphone.
"Windows protected your PC"? That message appears for new apps from small creators that aren't code-signed. Click More info → Run anyway. You only need to do this once.

Free plugins I use

  • Bertom Denoiser - removes background noise.
  • MeldaProduction free bundle - MCompressor, MEqualizer, MConvolutionEZ and more.
  • T-De-Esser 2 by Techivation - tames harsh S and T sounds.

You can use any VST3 plugins you like - these are just my go-tos.
